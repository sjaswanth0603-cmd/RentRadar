Regularization Engineering Rationale

  • Without Regularization (OLS): Ordinary Least Squares fits unconstrained coefficients, making it susceptible to variance amplification from high-cardinality one-hot encodings (e.g. cities and zip codes).
  • L2 Regularization (Ridge): Shrinks coefficients toward zero using a squared Euclidean weight penalty. This stabilizes collinear geographic indicators and yields superior out-of-sample generalization.
  • L1 Regularization (Lasso): Enforces sparsity via absolute magnitude penalty, strictly driving non-informative coefficients to 0.0. In this dataset, Lasso eliminated {{ results.lasso_zero_features }} redundant dimensions.
